!Tube - browse YouTube on RISC OS                         @VERSION@
===================================================================

Tube shows YouTube's videos in a desktop window, with their pictures,
and plays the one you click in Reel (riscos-ffmpeg), which uses the
Raspberry Pi's hardware decoder through ReelHWAccel. It is not made by
or connected with YouTube or Google.

It needs:
  - RISC OS 5.28 or later (the AcornSSL module, for https);
  - SharedUnixLibrary 1.16 or later and ARMEABISupport (PackMan);
  - Reel 0.1.27 or later: open the directory that has !Reel in it once,
    so the Filer has seen it (that sets up the *Reel command Tube uses);
  - a desktop of 32 thousand or 16 million colours (the pictures are
    plotted without a colour table).

Using it
--------
Double-click !Tube: its icon (a telly) appears on the icon bar. Click it
to open the window. Type what you want to watch in the box at the top
and press Return (or click Search). You can also paste a YouTube address
(youtube.com/watch?v=..., youtu.be/...): Tube plays that video and shows
the ones related to it.

In the window:
  Select on a video     plays it in Reel
  Adjust on a video     shows the videos related to it
  Select on the         shows that channel's videos
    channel's name
  Select on a channel   (in search results) its videos
  More (the last tile)  fetches the next part of the list
  Back                  the page before (up to 24 are kept)
  Menu                  Play, Related, Channel, Back, and Quality

Quality (on the menus) is the tallest video asked for: 360p, 480p,
720p or 1080p (the default). Tube asks YouTube for H.264 video, which
the Pi decodes in hardware up to 1080p, and AAC sound. Live streams are
played from YouTube's HLS playlists.

Reel is given a small file in <Wimp$ScrapDir> (TubePlay1 to 4) in the
same form as yt-dlp's -j output: the title, the two addresses and the
headers. Reel's own Media info window shows what's playing.

When it stops working
---------------------
YouTube changes what it expects from its apps every few months. Tube
copies the requests that yt-dlp and NewPipe make (October 2026). If
videos stop playing ("Sign in to confirm you're not a bot", or "no
H.264 streams"), newer client names and versions can be put in the
Config file inside !Tube (or a copy in Choices:Tube.Config, which wins)
without a new Tube; the file says where to find them.

The addresses YouTube gives work only from the internet connection that
asked for them, and for a few hours.

tubecli
-------
!Tube.tubecli is the same YouTube code for a TaskWindow, to try things
and find faults:
  tubecli search <words>          the results, as text
  tubecli related <video ID>
  tubecli channel <UC... ID>
  tubecli play <video ID> [lines] [file]   writes Reel's file
  tubecli -dump <directory> ...   keeps YouTube's raw answers
  tubecli -config <file> ...      reads settings as Config does
